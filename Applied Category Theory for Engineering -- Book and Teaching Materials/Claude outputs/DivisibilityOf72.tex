\begin{gradedsolution}
  The divisors of $72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$ are (in descending order) $\{72, 36, 24, 18, 12, 9, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1\}$.
  Each of them is of the form $2^i 3^j$ with $0 \leq i \leq 3$ and $0 \leq j \leq 2$, and an element is covered by another precisely when the latter is obtained from the former by multiplying with $2$ or with $3$.
  So the Hasse diagram is a $4 \times 3$ grid (here drawn rotated by $45^\circ$).
  Here is one possible way to draw it.
