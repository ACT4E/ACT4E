\subsection{Meta comments}

\begin{enumerate}
\item In general, we should watch out that on any given page or short sequence of pages there are not too many main messages being communicated at once. The main point should be clear, and larger disctractions from the main line of storytelling and explanation should be avoided. 
\item Generally it would be good to illustrate various of the ``simple mathematical examples'' with further, more concrete and applied examples -- in order to spell out the more concrete implications and possible uses of the mathematical examples. 
\item Generally, add additional concrete and applied examples that flesh out 'vanilla standard math examples', and show how the math concepts involved can take on an applied use (even if very simple use, this step from generic to context specific use is often a gap that is valuable (and often nontrivial) to bridge). 
\item
\end{enumerate}
