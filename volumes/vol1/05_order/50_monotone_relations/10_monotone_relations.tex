\section{Monotone relations}


Just like we generalized functions to relations, we can generalize monotone functions to monotone relations. 


\begin{ctdefinition}[monotone relation]
Let $\posA= \tup{\posAset, \posleq_\posA}$ and~$\posB = \tup{\posBset, \posleq_\posB}$ be preorders. 

A monotone relation $\relA \colon \posA \mto \posB$ is:

\constit
\begin{enumerate}
\item a relation $\relA \colon \posAset \mto \posBset$ 
\end{enumerate}
\condit
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\tup{\ela, \elb} \in \relA, \quad \ela' \posleq_\funsp \ela \ \implies \tup{\ela', \elb} \in \relA$;
\item $\tup{\ela, \elb} \in \relA, \quad \elb \posleq_\ressp \elb' \ \implies \tup{\ela, \elb'} \in \relA$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{ctdefinition}
